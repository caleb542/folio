Bio

Full stack developer with twelve years delivering pixel perfect digital products in environments where things can't be broken — national telecoms, enterprise CMS, financial services.

I work front-end and full cycle: interface build, CMS architecture, back-end integration, cross-browser and responsive QA, HTML email, accessibility, client management, and cross-department asset coordination.

At Bell Canada I was auditing completed tickets for accessibility issues when I uncovered a series of interconnected failures across the entire Virgin Plus Member Benefits enrollment flow — unreachable legal disclosures that mouse and touch users would never notice, invisible only to those navigating by keyboard alone, broken focus management, and untrapped modals. Every offer on the page was effectively unusable for keyboard and screen reader users. I scoped the fixes, wrote the tickets, assigned them to myself, and saw it through to live.

Currently building Recipe Me — a front-end recipe platform and a demonstration of how I work when nobody is asking me to build anything.

Outside of code: musician, and a culinary background that probably explains why Recipe Me exists.

Experience

Bell Canada – Lead developer for the Virgin Plus Member Benefits microsite, supporting bell.ca and virginplus.ca — two of Canada's highest-traffic consumer websites.

As part of my role I spent time auditing recently completed tickets for accessibility issues, testing work across the team and flagging defects for remediation. During that process I uncovered a series of interconnected failures across the entire enrollment flow.

Every offer on the page had a "Good to Know" disclosure — legal terms covering eligibility, dates, and limitations. The anchor had no href, making it completely invisible to keyboard users — unreachable legal content that mouse and touch users would never notice. Beyond that, enrollment forms opened in a lightbox but focus stayed behind, and with no focus trapping, tabbing through would drop you back out into the page. Some forms contained nested modals with additional terms. None of it was managed.

I scoped the fixes, wrote the tickets, assigned them to myself, and worked through the remediation across multiple sprints — implementing semantic modal architecture with focus trapping and focus restoration throughout the enrollment flow. Reviewed and approved by my senior developer and manager. Shipped with no issues.

Beyond that: delivered 200+ promotional campaigns, designed and tested responsive HTML emails across 15+ clients using Litmus, conducted AODA and WCAG 2.0 audits remediating 50+ violations, and trained 8+ team members on accessibility standards and content workflows.

Bruze Inc. – Eleven years at a digital studio building enterprise CMS platforms for clients in financial services, investment management, and technology — including Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan, Franklin Templeton, Russell Investments, IA Clarington, and BlackBerry.

Built MyPensionHub (mypensionhub.com / carrefourretraite.com) — a fully bilingual English and French document and resource repository serving multiple Ontario pension organizations. A standalone LAMP stack application using PHP, Smarty templating, and a custom MySQL database. Still live today.

Key platforms included Liferay 6.1 and 6.2 for OTPP's public corporate website and intranet, and Liferay 7 DXP for the Bruze rebrand. OTPP ultimately migrated to Adobe Experience Manager, a transition I supported through the handoff period.
